## Vertical-specific tips
- **Caption safe zone:** keep the bottom ~15% and top ~10% of the frame clear — that's where Reels/Shorts UI (username, buttons, progress bar) sits. The prompts already push the action to center.
- **Screen recordings (R1/R2/R3):** record on a normal wide screen, then in the editor **zoom into the terminal region** and stack vertically — a wide terminal shrunk into a 9:16 frame is unreadable. Crop to the chat bubble / the recall line so it stays legible on a phone.
- **Cut length for vertical:** Shorts/Reels/TikTok reward 7–15s. Use the **silent captioned Cut A** here (the loop), not the 60s narrated version. The 60s narrated Cut B stays 16:9 for YouTube/homepage.
- **One render, two ratios:** if credits are tight, you can generate 16:9 only and do a center-crop to 9:16 in the editor — cheaper, slightly looser framing. Generating native 9:16 (these prompts) looks better but costs the extra clips. Your call based on budget.
